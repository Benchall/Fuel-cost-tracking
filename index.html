Getting these figures into wages and the P&L

Driver Pay Log: “Download for Driver Pay Log” gives one line per driver per day with a deduction, as Fuel over allowance (a charge off pay). If you pay the home allowance through wages, home-charged days come through as Top up. Driver names here must match the names in the pay log.

P&L Tracker: “Download for P&L” gives the cost to the business for each driver for each day (£10 on a home-charged day, up to £20 on a public day). Put it in the Charging £ box on the P&L's daily entry.
