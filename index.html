Getting these figures into wages and the P&L

Driver Pay Log: “Download for Driver Pay Log” gives one line per driver per day: deductions as Fuel over allowance (a charge off pay) and, if you pay the home allowance through wages, home days as Top up. Add them under “Add a charge or payment”. Driver names here must match the names in the pay log.

P&L Tracker: “Download for P&L” gives the cost to the business for each FD for each day. Put it in the Charging £ box on the P&L's daily entry. It already takes off what drivers pay back and includes the home allowance, so don't count the home allowance again in wages on the P&L.
